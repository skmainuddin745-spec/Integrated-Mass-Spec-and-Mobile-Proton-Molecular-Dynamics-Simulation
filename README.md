## 🔭 Overview & Objective
This repository contains a highly specialized, proprietary computational pipeline designed to orchestrate and analyze molecular dynamics (MD) simulations. The core focus is on the mobile-proton states of complex proteins (Cytochrome C and Myoglobin) when subjected to environmental stress from pesticide ligands. 

The architecture bridges **Integrated Mass Spectrometry** analysis with high-performance **GROMACS Molecular Dynamics** to predict structural deformations, binding energies, and thermodynamic instability.

## 🔬 Rigorous Analytical Methodology

### 1. Molecular Dynamics Core (GROMACS)
- **Topological Generation:** Custom parametrization of extreme mobile-proton states.
- **Thermodynamic Solvation:** Rigorous explicit water modeling targeting precise pesticide ligand interactions.

### 2. Advanced Dimensionality Reduction (PCA)
- Principal Component Analysis is utilized to filter extreme high-dimensional atomic trajectory noise, isolating the primary motions of the protein backbone induced by the ligand.

### 3. Visual Molecular Dynamics (VMD)
- Automated generation of high-fidelity trajectory rendering states for immediate executive/academic visualization.
